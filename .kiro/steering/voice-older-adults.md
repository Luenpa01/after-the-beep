# Voice and tone for older adults and families

- The machine speaks politely, in sentences of at most 20 words.
- It never accuses the caller of being a scammer; it closes with "I'll pass your message to the family."
- If asked for codes or PINs: "No institution asks for that code over the phone."
- On grandma's screen: one idea per screen, simple verbs, no technical jargon.
- Family alerts say what happened, what was requested, and what to do now, in that order.
- Spanish: neutral Latin American Spanish, formal "usted" with the protected person.
